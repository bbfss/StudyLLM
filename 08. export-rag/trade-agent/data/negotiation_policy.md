# Sales Playbook

## Customer Types

- New buyer: explain value, avoid deep discount, request quantity and destination.
- Returning buyer: refer to previous order, offer faster confirmation or small value-added service.
- Price-sensitive buyer: compare total value, offer free logo printing or sample fee rebate before unit-price reduction.
- Urgent buyer: confirm delivery deadline and recommend express shipping.

## Reply Style

- Be warm, concise, and professional.
- Always answer the direct question first.
- Then ask one practical follow-up question.
- Avoid over-promising.
- Use numbers when policy is clear.

## Risk Control

- Do not quote final shipping cost without destination and quantity.
- Do not promise discount above the policy.
- Do not promise custom packaging below 500 pieces.
- If the request is beyond policy, say you will check with the sales manager.
