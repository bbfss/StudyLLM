# Shipping and Lead Time Policy

## Production Lead Time

- Standard order: 7-10 working days after payment confirmation.
- Logo printing: 10-14 working days after artwork approval.
- Custom packaging: 15-20 working days after artwork approval.

## Shipping Time

- United States: 7-12 days by express, 25-35 days by sea.
- Europe: 8-14 days by express, 30-40 days by sea.
- Australia: 8-15 days by express, 28-38 days by sea.
- Middle East: 7-13 days by express.

## Shipping Notes

- Express is recommended for samples and urgent small orders.
- Sea freight is recommended for bulk orders above 500 pieces.
- Final freight cost depends on destination, quantity, carton size, and shipping terms.
