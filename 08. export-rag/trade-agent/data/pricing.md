# Pricing and Negotiation Policy

## Base Price

- 100-299 pieces: USD 12.80 per piece
- 300-499 pieces: USD 11.90 per piece
- 500-999 pieces: USD 10.80 per piece
- 1000+ pieces: USD 9.90 per piece

## MOQ

- Standard product MOQ: 100 pieces
- Logo printing MOQ: 300 pieces
- Custom packaging MOQ: 500 pieces

## Discount Rules

- Do not offer more than 3% extra discount for first-time buyers.
- For 500+ pieces, it is acceptable to offer free logo printing instead of lowering unit price.
- For 1000+ pieces, escalate to sales manager if the customer asks for additional discount.
- If the customer focuses only on price, ask about target quantity, destination country, and expected delivery date before making a concession.

## Safe Negotiation Guidance

- Start by confirming order quantity and destination.
- Prefer value-added concessions such as free logo printing, sample fee rebate, or faster production update.
- Never promise a final price without confirming shipping terms.
