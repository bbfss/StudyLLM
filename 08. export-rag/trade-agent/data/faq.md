# FAQ and Service Policy

## Samples

- Samples are available for quality checking.
- Sample price is USD 25 per unit.
- Sample fee can be refunded when the first bulk order reaches 300 pieces.
- Sample preparation usually takes 2-3 working days.

## Warranty

- Warranty period: 12 months from delivery.
- Defective units can be replaced in the next order after photo or video confirmation.
- Damage caused by misuse is not covered.

## Payment

- Standard payment term: 30% deposit and 70% balance before shipment.
- For samples, 100% payment is required before shipment.

## Complaint Handling

- Ask for order number, photos, videos, and quantity of defective units.
- Do not admit liability before checking evidence.
- Offer replacement in the next shipment if the defect is confirmed.
